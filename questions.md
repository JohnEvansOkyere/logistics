# Questions about your logistics operations

Hello,

I have put together a few questions to make sure the system follows how your team actually works. Please reply using the question numbers; short answers are fine. If a question is easier to explain in a call, just write **“let's discuss.”** You can also ask the colleague who handles that part of the work to answer it.

We are planning **email and SMS** for customer updates in the first release. **WhatsApp can be added later**; there is nothing you need to set up for it now.

## Jobs and daily work

1. Which services do you want to start using in the system first: sea imports, sea exports, air imports, air exports, warehouse storage, or standalone road transport? Should the remaining services follow in later stages?
2. For a typical **sea import**, **sea export**, **air import**, and **air export**, what are the main steps your team follows? Which documents or confirmations must be present before a job is considered finished? Please mention any steps that are different from the process you already described.
3. If a job is cancelled, delayed, delivered with damage, or needs to be reopened after closure, who decides what happens next? Can any required document or step be skipped, and who may approve that?
4. When can a job be opened: only after a customer accepts a quotation, or also when a customer gives instructions directly? How do you record that a quotation has been accepted? Do you have an existing format for job/file numbers or old records you want to bring into the system?

## Quotations, invoices, and payment records

5. Who prepares and approves quotations and invoices? Should the system **create your official invoices**, or only store invoices prepared elsewhere? Can you share blank or safely redacted examples of a quotation and invoice?
6. What currencies do you quote and invoice in? How do you handle exchange rates, VAT or other taxes, due dates, discounts, and corrected or cancelled invoices? Your accounts person can help answer this.
7. Staff will record money received **outside** the system; customers will not pay through the system. Who may add or correct a payment record? Do you accept part-payments, and what proof or reference must be kept?

## People, customer access, and paperwork

8. Which staff members should be able to create jobs, update shipments, approve extracted document details, issue invoices, record payments, and close jobs? Who should approve exceptions?
9. What should customers be allowed to see themselves: quotations, arrival dates, job progress, invoices, waybills, delivery notes, other documents, or anything else? Are there details that must stay visible to staff only? Can one customer contact represent more than one company?
10. Could you share **blank or safely redacted** examples of your waybill, delivery note/proof of delivery, and empty-container return/EIR? What signatures and reference numbers must appear on each?
11. Which documents do you most want the system to read and suggest details from first (for example, bills of lading or air waybills)? Which details should staff check before saving them? Are you comfortable with an outside document-reading service processing authorised samples, or should we discuss that first?

## Tracking and customer messages

12. Which shipping lines and airlines do you use most? How does your team currently obtain and confirm arrival/departure dates? If automatic updates are not available from a carrier, is a staff-entered update acceptable?
13. Which events should trigger a customer **email**, and which should trigger an **SMS** (for example: quote ready, arrival date changed, customs release, delivery arranged, job completed)? Who should receive them, and should staff approve a message before it is sent?
14. Which email address and SMS sender name/number should customers see? Do you already use an SMS service? How do customers agree to receive SMS, and are there hours when messages should not be sent?
15. Do you need past email or SMS conversations brought into the system, or is it enough to record new messages and allow staff to note important past conversations manually?

## Warehouse, road transport, and records

16. For warehouse work, how do you record goods received, storage location, units/quantities, partial collections, damage, storage charges, and stock reports? For standalone road transport, which trip, driver, vehicle, and delivery details must be recorded?
17. Roughly how many staff users, customer users, new jobs, and uploaded documents do you expect per month? Do you have a deadline or budget range for the first release?
18. How long do you need to keep completed jobs, invoices, and documents? Are there requirements about where customer data is stored or how quickly you must regain access after a system outage? If your accountant or adviser handles these rules, who should we speak to?

Thank you. We can begin setting up the system while you consider these questions. Your answers will help us tailor the workflows and paperwork, and agree on what is ready for your team to use in the first release.
