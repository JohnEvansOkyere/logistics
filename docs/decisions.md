## Confirmed access model — D04 (2026-09-28)

- The `super_admin` creates user accounts directly in the admin section, sets their initial password, and assigns the appropriate role(s). The system does not send invitations or credentials by email; the administrator gives the login details to the staff member. No fixed staff names are required for workflow roles.
- The `super_admin` can replace an account password and suspend/reactivate accounts. Password replacement can let another person use the same account; prior and future activity remains attributed to that account and cannot distinguish the people who used it. Suspension removes active staff roles and blocks sign-in while preserving the account and role history.
- Customer access is determined by explicit company membership. Users can see all business records belonging to their assigned company or companies; they cannot access another company's records, including by guessing a record ID. There is no separate customer-visible document-type list or publication gate.
- D04's business rules are confirmed. API-level authorization and cross-company denial still require implementation and automated verification; those are build tasks, not open questions.

## Confirmed quote and operations decisions (2026-09-28)

- **D03 — configuration, not assumed legal/tax advice:** The app must not hard-code BJH's legal issuer status, tax treatment, currencies or related finance rules. Authorized BJH users should be able to configure them. Until configured and validated by BJH, the app must not claim a quote or invoice has legal/tax status. This decision does not authorize implementing invoice issuance in the quote-draft slice.
- **D06 — departmental preparation and super-admin approval:** The representative responsible for a department prepares its operational records and documents; the super admin reviews and approves them before they are finalized or issued. Quote drafts remain drafts until that approval/issuance workflow is implemented. Existing `EVIDENCES/` documents are structural references only; preserve client data and use synthetic values in development.
- **D12 — acceptance and imports:** Do not import historic records. A customer acceptance is the acceptance of the identified quote/version itself; no separate signature or evidence attachment is required by this decision. Record which quote/version was accepted, who recorded it and when for traceability. Job-number format and staff-created jobs without quotes remain undecided.

## D06 — supplied document structure observations

The user confirms that the layouts in `EVIDENCES/` are structural references for the future BJH documents, and that the payment receipt should be a conventional receipt with customer details. These notes describe layout only; use synthetic values in any implementation fixture and do not copy the client-specific data. A structural blueprint is recorded in [document-templates.md](document-templates.md).

- `invoice.pdf` is a third-party port-services export invoice, not a BJH-issued invoice. Its layout includes issuer/customer and shipment references, vessel/booking/container details, charge line items, tax/levy breakdown, currency/total, container details, payment instructions, and e-invoice status.
- `quote_import_clearance_by_sea.pdf` and `BJH_Logistics_Tema_Port_Clearance_Quotation.pdf` show charge tables by container size and basis (fixed/per bill/at cost), currency, scope, required documents, expected timeline, exceptions/terms, and acceptance signatures.
- `Transport_bill.pdf` and `HBL - 125-23823984.pdf` are bill-of-lading layouts with shipper/consignee/notify parties, routing, master/house references, cargo, packages, container/seal, weights, freight terms, and issue/signature fields. `BJH_Air_Cargo_Manifest_125-23823984.pdf` shows the corresponding master/house air-waybill shipment summary.
- `prov_of_delivery.pdf` is proof of cargo delivery with an AWB reference, consignee, goods, packages/weight, date/time, recipient name/phone/signature, and carrier stamp. It is not a cash receipt. No standalone customer payment receipt or its photo is present in the local supplied files; `invoice.pdf` says the bank issues a receipt after payment.

The structural notes above do not authorize copying client-specific values or generating legal/financial documents. User-configurable finance settings and the super-admin approval workflow still need implementation and acceptance before issuance.

## Confirmed decisions (2026-09-29, Evans)

- **Direct jobs (closes the open part of D12):** The normal path is quote sent to the client → client accepts → staff open the job. Existing clients can also have a job opened directly with no quote. Who may open a direct job is not yet confirmed.
- **Single database adapter:** Retire the local SQLite adapter and its migrations. API tests run against local Supabase PostgreSQL; `supabase/migrations/` is the only schema source. Deleting the SQLite code still needs confirmation at the time of the change.
- **Warehousing and road transport scope (narrows D10):** BJH does light, regular warehousing (not high-volume inventory) and simple driver/truck assignment. Build simple records rather than a full stock-management module. Whether these get dedicated staff roles is not yet confirmed.
- **Who opens direct jobs:** Users with any active staff role can open a job directly without a quote. Assumption until confirmed: department reps open jobs for their own service line, and the super admin can open any.
- **Warehouse and road roles:** Existing staff manage warehousing; no separate warehouse role. Road transport work is assigned to a driver. Drivers do not sign in; they are records (name and phone) that staff assign to a trip.
- **Customer accounts (extends D04):** The super admin creates customer-user accounts the same way as staff accounts (admin-set password, no invitation) and links them to their company.
